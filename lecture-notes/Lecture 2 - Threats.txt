Components of a Threat
1. Threat Agent
2. Capability
3. Threat Amplifiers
4. Threat Catalyst
5. Threat Agent Motivators

Storyline, the threat usually have an actor, then they have the opportunity, motivation capability to do threat. But why? Because they have the motivation to do so and caused by catalyst (something that triggers the actor to do the threat), amplifier, and inhibitor.

Component of a Threat
1. Threat Agents
- The people who would do the threat (Terrorist, Hackers)
2. Capability
- Software, Technology, Training Methods, Books and Manuals
3. Threat Amplifiers (Making action more likely)
- Peer pressure, perception, access to information, education level
4. Threat Catalyst (Triggering action)
- Events, technology change, personal circumstances
5. Threat Agent Motivators
- Political, secular, personal gain, religion, power, terrorism, and curiosity

Top 10 Categories of threat
1. Malware - software that can cause damage (Virus, Trojan)
2. Ransonware - software that does not damage the system and they will ask for your money
3. Phishing & Email Attacks - hook links through email, phising is general attack, spear-phishing is a specific attack
4. Social Engineering - scam calls and then messages
5. Password & Credential Attacks - brute force is a type of attack to guess the password, dictionary attack (common password list)
6. Network Attacks - Packet Sniffing
7. Denial of Service Attacks - DoS, DDoS make the requests to many times to a server and this breaksdown the webpage
8. Web Application Attacks - SQL Injection, Cross Site Scripting
9. Insider Threats - Malicious Employee, Negligent User, Data Theft
10. Advanced Persistent Threats - Nation-state espionage, long term attacks
