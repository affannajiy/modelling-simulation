Boids Reynold Boids simulated the animal flocking purely by modelling individual behaviour. It showed the combination of these inexpensive, local ineractions yields rich emergent group behaviours.

The simulations by Boids, follow a simple agents based model for realistic flocking in animation where each of them are autonomous boid that follows three local steering rules.
1. Separation (avoid crowding neighbours)
2. Alignment (match the velocity with nearby voids)
3. Cohesion (steer towards the neighbours center)
Plus they may have obstacle avoidance and seeking the goal.
